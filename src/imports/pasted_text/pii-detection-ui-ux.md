Design a premium, modern, aesthetic web application UI/UX prototype for the Smart India Hackathon problem statement:

PS2608 — Creating an application to identify the presence of government-issued Personally Identifiable Information (PII) embedded in documents and data.

The application is an AI-powered PII Detection & Protection platform that can scan uploaded documents/data and detect sensitive information such as Aadhaar numbers, PAN numbers, Driving Licence details, names, phone numbers, addresses, dates of birth, email IDs and other sensitive PII.

1. Overall Design Style

Create a design that looks like it was made by a professional UI/UX designer and a cybersecurity product company, not a basic student dashboard.

Theme

- Primary theme: Elegant brown / warm coffee aesthetic
- Use shades like:
  - Deep espresso brown
  - Dark chocolate
  - Warm beige
  - Cream / ivory
  - Subtle golden/tan accents
- Use a clean light/dark combination where appropriate.
- Soft gradients, glassmorphism and subtle shadows are welcome.
- Avoid making the interface too dark or too flashy.
- Keep it premium, minimal, trustworthy and futuristic.

Visual Style

Use:

- Clean typography
- Rounded cards
- Soft shadows
- Subtle animations
- Modern icons
- Small cybersecurity-inspired elements
- 3D illustrations related to privacy, documents, AI scanning, security shields, identity protection and data masking.

The 3D illustrations should look sophisticated and integrated into the UI rather than random decorative images.

---

“Privacy AI Assistant”

“Hi! I can help you detect, understand and protect sensitive information in your documents.”

The assistant should support:

- Multiple languages
- Explaining detected PII
- Explaining why specific information is sensitive
- Answering whether certain information is necessary
- Suggesting what should be masked
- Guiding users through redaction
- Explaining privacy risks

“What should I redact?”

“Is this information necessary?”

“Explain the risk”

18. FINAL VISUAL DIRECTION

The final design should look like a real AI cybersecurity/privacy SaaS product that could realistically be presented at a national-level hackathon.

It should NOT look like:

- A generic admin dashboard
- A basic Bootstrap website
- A simple college project
- An overcrowded government portal

It should look like:

“A premium AI-powered privacy protection platform.”

Use the brown + cream + subtle gold aesthetic consistently throughout the entire application.

Use 3D illustrations selectively, especially on:

- Landing page
- Document upload
- AI scanning
- Government ID recognition
- Security/protection screens

Keep the first page minimal and visually impressive.

Most importantly, prioritize UI/UX, usability, visual hierarchy and professional product presentation over putting excessive information on every screen.
